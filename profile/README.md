A hands-on view of the world's most important industry: AI and semiconductors.

The most important problem of the century is building intelligence too cheap to meter. That takes capable software, accessible hardware designs, reliable benchmarks, and a clear understanding of the industry.

Semihack brings these efforts together: post-training, quantization, serving engines, and hardware work extending toward tapeout, alongside news and technical analysis. We’re building an open ecosystem that spans the full stack, so anyone can access the work, contribute to it, and take it further.

Our repositories are starting points for that work:

- [Quantization](https://github.com/semihack/quantization): model quantization and evaluation.
- [Simulators](https://github.com/semihack/simulators): tools for understanding hardware behavior.
- [Accelerators](https://github.com/semihack/accelerators): experiments in AI accelerator design.
- [Lectures](https://github.com/semihack/lectures): teaching material and learning notes.
