A hands-on view of the world's most important industry: AI and semiconductors.

We explore how to make AI run faster and more efficiently, from algorithms and inference software to chip architecture and device technology. Code, experiments, and technical writing are how we work through those questions.

Our repositories are starting points for that work:

- [Quantization](https://github.com/semihack/quantization): model quantization and evaluation.
- [Simulators](https://github.com/semihack/simulators): tools for understanding hardware behavior.
- [Accelerators](https://github.com/semihack/accelerators): experiments in AI accelerator design.
- [Lectures](https://github.com/semihack/lectures): teaching material and learning notes.
